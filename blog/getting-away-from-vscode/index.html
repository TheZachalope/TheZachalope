No More Manual Site Code and Other Updates

Zachalope September 2, 2026

Well, I've decided to got away from just coding the website myself at all and am going with a static CMS generator. I thought about using Hugo, but didn't really care for it / have the time to learn it. Instead, at the time of this post, I'm converting my website into Publii. It's free and clean. Had to manually move my blog posts over, but that didn't take too long. I think I have everything set up, but I'm sure I'll discover something that needs tweaked or added later on... I actually like the default "Simple" theme the most of the options I looked at, so here we are. 

Life has been... full of chaos and change. I've moved into the house up the hill from my parents and am currently boxing up/cleaning my old house before I can sell it. I've had to buy all new household items, but I think I'm about fully set up. Just need to organize the things I have better and get a storage unit for the things in my old house I'm not taking.

My two daughters are doing well. Happy and healthy, for the most part. I love getting greeted by running hugs when I get home! They love the new place. Probaby helps that they can see Grandma and Grandpa's house just down the hill from our porches. I'm doing pretty well, too. Still have a lot of work to do, but it is what it is. 

I may start randomly streaming on Twitch. If I do, I imagine it'd only be like once or twice a month and quality would be a little odd. I'd be using the Steam Link app on my old rugged laptop to stream the audo/video to OBS from my Steam Deck and using Veadotube Mini to have my little icon indicate when I speak. So definitely a little janky. But could be fun! May post more on TikTok, too. Who knows. I'll have a little more free time than I'm used to once I get the moving pains / house selling out of the way.
