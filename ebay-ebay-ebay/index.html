eBay eBay eBay

Zachalope June 24, 2025

Well, my wife's eBay store is going well. Defintely going to be a viable thing for her to keep going, just need to figure out organization a little better. That's like a plague in our house currently... but we'll get there! Anyway, she takes nice pictures and makes her eBay listings look very well. Resaling is something she didn't think she's ever do, but it's become fun for her.

My eBay, on the other hand, will be a slow burn. I've sold a couple of things, but not for much profit. Still, it's fun to list. Good news is I was able to change my username to just "zachalope," so I'm pretty happy about that. I'd like to focus more on electronic stuff, but I'll sell things my wife doesn't think fit her aesthetic as well. Currently I have 5 iPad keyboard cases listed. Would be cool if they sold.

I also bought a couple of things on eBay recently. One of which is a Dell Inspirion Mini 10 netbook. I had thought I could use it around the house for a tiny laptop to update the blog on, but it only runs x32 bit things and also has issues with the keyboard... It's not worth fixing to me, so I'll just list it back on eBay later on. The other things I bought are for work - a sit stand desk converter and dual monitor mounts to use with it.
I'll have to completely re-arrange my area, but I know I'll utilize standing up a lot at work. Plus, the desk converter was only $60 on sale when I couldn't even find a decent used one local for that price.

Anyway, if you decide you want to check out my wife's or my shops, there's Crimson Cardinal Skincare, Crimson Cardinal Products on eBay, and then my eBay. I don't have an actual storefront there. Maybe if things kick off, I'll pay to get one. My wife does, though. She takes pride in her storefronts and wants to make sure everything is good quality.

Everything else in my life is essentially the same. A lot of projects needing to get done, work, kids, etc. Life is life, but life can be fun.
